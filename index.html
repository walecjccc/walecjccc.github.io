简介About

我是青岛科技大学数据科学学院大数据方向的研究生（2026 级），在 "AI for Engineering" 团队从事工业人工智能相关研究，导师为王殿辉教授。 I am a graduate student in Big Data at the College of Data Science, Qingdao University of Science and Technology (Class of 2026). I work on industrial artificial intelligence in the "AI for Engineering" group, advised by Prof. Dianhui Wang.

我的关注点在于：如何把轻量、可解释的机器学习模型用到真实工业场景里——尤其是那些"很少发生、但一旦发生代价很高"的关键事件，例如制造过程中的质量缺陷与设备异常。目前在复现并拓展随机配置网络（SCN / SCM）相关理论，并在轮胎制造场景中做验证。 My interest is in bringing lightweight and interpretable machine learning into real industrial settings — especially rare-but-costly critical events such as manufacturing quality defects and equipment anomalies. I am currently reproducing and extending Stochastic Configuration Networks (SCN / SCM) and validating them in tire-manufacturing scenarios.

  • 小概率重要事件预测：面向类别极不平衡场景的建模方法 Prediction of rare yet critical events: modeling under extreme class imbalance

  • 轻量化与可解释性：低算力条件下的可解释工业智能模型 Lightweight & interpretable models: interpretable industrial AI under low-compute constraints
